On 28 August 2026, Saudi Mecha Robot celebrated its second anniversary.

## Highlights

- **Group build challenge** with club members.
- **Beginner workshop** for people starting the hobby.
- **Appreciation certificates** for members.

Thank you to every member who has been part of the club over the past two years.
