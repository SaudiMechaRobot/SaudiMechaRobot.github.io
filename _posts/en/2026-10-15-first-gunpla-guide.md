If this is your first time building Gunpla, this guide gets you started quickly.

## Which grade should I start with?

| Grade | Scale | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| HG (High Grade) | 1/144 | The best start: easy and affordable |
| RG (Real Grade) | 1/144 | Builders who know HG and want finer detail |
| MG (Master Grade) | 1/100 | Has an inner frame and takes longer |
| PG (Perfect Grade) | 1/60 | For experienced builders, the largest and most complex grade |

## Basic tools

- **Nippers:** to cut parts off the runner without breaking them.
- **Fine file or sanding paper:** to smooth the cut marks.
- **Hobby knife:** to remove small leftovers.
