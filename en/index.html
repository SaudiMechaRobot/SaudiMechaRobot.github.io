01 · ABOUT

About the club

Saudi Mecha Robot was founded in Riyadh in {{ site.founded }}. It is a meeting place for model kit builders across Saudi Arabia who love mecha anime, Gunpla and Japanese pop culture.

Our goal is to grow the model-building hobby among young Saudis and to act as a cultural bridge between Saudi Arabia and Japan through mecha.
